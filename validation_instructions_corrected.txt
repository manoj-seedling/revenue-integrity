PURPOSE
You are reviewing 13 claims extracted from 12 synthetic Salesforce opportunities.
Your feedback will help us evaluate:
1. Claim Detection Correctness
2. Evidence Policy Appropriateness
3. Proposed State Correctness
4. Business Usefulness

You are not confirming what actually happened in a commercial deal. You are
evaluating whether the system's interpretation and classification follow the
evidence policy and whether the output is operationally useful.

THE FOUR REVIEW QUESTIONS

1. CLAIM DETECTION CORRECT
   Options: YES / NO / PARTIAL
   YES: The claim was correctly extracted from the note.
   NO: The claim was incorrectly extracted, has the wrong type, or is absent.
   PARTIAL: The extraction is partly correct but needs adjustment.

2. EVIDENCE POLICY APPROPRIATE
   Options: YES / NO / CANNOT_DETERMINE
   YES: The evidence required for this claim type is appropriate.
   NO: The evidence policy is too narrow or inappropriate for this claim type.
   CANNOT_DETERMINE: There is not enough context to judge.

3. PROPOSED STATE CORRECT
   Options: YES / NO / CANNOT_DETERMINE
   YES: The proposed state correctly follows the configured policy.
   NO: The state should be different given the evidence.
   CANNOT_DETERMINE: There is not enough context to judge.

4. BUSINESS USEFUL
   Options: YES / NO / UNSURE
   YES: This exception would be useful in a forecast review.
   NO: This exception would not be actionable.
   UNSURE: You are unsure about its usefulness.

SYSTEM DECISION STATES

SUPPORTED: Available CRM evidence satisfies the configured requirement.
UNSUPPORTED: A claim exists but required supporting evidence is absent.
CONTRADICTED: Available evidence actively conflicts with the claim.
NOT_VERIFIABLE: The configured data cannot test the claim reliably.

REASON CODES
Choose one if any question is answered NO or PARTIAL:

CLAIM_NOT_PRESENT: The claim does not exist in the source text.
WRONG_CLAIM_TYPE: The claim exists but has the wrong type.
EVIDENCE_NOT_CAPTURED: Evidence exists but was not captured by the policy.
EVIDENCE_RULE_INCORRECT: The evidence rule is wrong or incomplete.
WRONG_DECISION_STATE: The classification does not follow the policy.
INSUFFICIENT_CONTEXT: There is not enough information to evaluate.
VALID_BUT_NOT_ACTIONABLE: The result is correct but not useful in practice.
OTHER: Another reason; explain it in Reviewer_Notes.

HOW TO RECORD YOUR REVIEW

For each assigned row in reviews.csv, complete:
- Review_Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Claim_Detection_Correct: YES / NO / PARTIAL
- Evidence_Policy_Appropriate: YES / NO / CANNOT_DETERMINE
- Proposed_State_Correct: YES / NO / CANNOT_DETERMINE
- Corrected_Claim_Type: only when correction is needed
- Corrected_State: SUPPORTED / UNSUPPORTED / CONTRADICTED / NOT_VERIFIABLE
- Business_Useful: YES / NO / UNSURE
- Primary_Reason_Code: required when an issue is identified
- Reviewer_Notes: brief explanation, normally 2-3 sentences
- Reviewer_Confidence: 1-5, where 1 is low and 5 is high

IMPORTANT NOTES

1. This does not measure real-world precision. It measures reviewer acceptance
   under synthetic evaluation scenarios.
2. All proposed states are UNSUPPORTED because the records show missing
   supporting evidence, not actively opposing evidence.
3. Each claim is reviewed separately. Some opportunities have multiple claims.
4. Complete the review within 48 hours. Estimated time: 2-3 minutes per claim.
5. Reviewers must work independently and must not share answers until both
   reviews are submitted.
